# Support

PgDesigner is free and open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), including commercial use.

## Community

- Bug reports and feature requests: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/vmkteam/pgdesigner/issues)
- Questions and ideas: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/vmkteam/pgdesigner/discussions)

Community support is best-effort, with no guaranteed response time.

## Paid support

For teams that depend on PgDesigner:

| Plan | Price | Details |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Priority Support** | $149/year per company | Private email support, response within 2 business days, your bug reports go first, help with migration from pgModeler, MicroOLAP or DbSchema |
| **Enterprise Support** | from $990/year | Response within 1 business day, private chat channel, onboarding call, roadmap priority, invoice/PO |
| **Sponsored development** | custom quote | A feature or importer you need, built into the open-source release |

Details and checkout: [pgdesigner.io/pricing](https://pgdesigner.io/pricing). Contact: [support@pgdesigner.io](mailto:support@pgdesigner.io).
